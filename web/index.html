King County measles exposure sites

Under 12 months: babies haven't had their routine MMR dose. If your baby was at a listed place and time, call your pediatrician right away. Immune globulin (IG) given within 6 days of exposure can prevent or soften measles. Call ahead; don't walk into a clinic or ER with a possibly exposed baby.
